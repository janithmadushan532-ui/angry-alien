RELIC RUN v1.6.2
================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
2. Double-click "Relic Run.html". It opens in your web browser.
   (If it opens in something else, right-click it > Open with > Chrome, Edge or Firefox.)

No install and no internet connection needed. Sound starts after your first click.

Controls
  Steer / turn ....... Left / Right arrows or A / D
  Jump ............... Up arrow, W or Space
  Slide .............. Down arrow, S or Shift
  Pause .............. Esc or P
  A standard gamepad also works.

Your progress (coins, explorers, upgrades, records) is saved by your browser
for this file. Keep the file in the same place and keep using the same
browser to keep your progress. Moving the file, using a different browser,
or clearing browsing data starts a fresh save.

Needs a modern browser with hardware acceleration turned on for the full 3D
graphics. Without it the game falls back to simpler graphics.
